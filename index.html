ConsentAlly demo stores

Two copies of one store page, each with the ConsentAlly banner and Google Consent Mode. On these two, the banner shows for every visitor, in every region.

  • gtag.js store: the ConsentAlly install tag, then gtag.js for Google Analytics 4.
  • Tag Manager store: a Google Tag Manager container whose ConsentAlly tag loads the banner.

One more store, on its own address, shows a banner only in some regions: regional store, where the banner asks first in the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and no banner shows in the United States, where Google Consent Mode is updated to granted as the page loads.
